# Communication and Listening Skills

## Question 1

### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)

### Answer

* **Pay full attention** – Focus completely on the person who is speaking and avoid distractions.
* **Maintain eye contact** – Look at the speaker to show that you are interested and listening.
* **Do not interrupt** – Allow the speaker to finish before giving your response.
* **Understand the message** – Listen carefully to understand not only the words but also the speaker's meaning and feelings.
* **Ask questions** – Ask relevant questions when something is unclear or needs more explanation.
* **Give feedback** – Respond appropriately using words or gestures such as nodding to show that you understand.
* **Show empathy** – Try to understand the speaker's feelings and point of view.
* **Repeat or summarize** – Briefly repeat the main points in your own words to confirm that you understood correctly.

## Question 2

### According to Fisher's Model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

### Answer

* **Listen carefully** – Pay attention to what the person is saying.
* **Understand the message** – Try to understand what the person really means.
* **Repeat in your own words** – Tell the person what you understood in simple words.
* **Understand their feelings** – Pay attention to how the person is feeling.
* **Ask questions if you are confused** – If you don't understand something, ask the person to explain it.
* **Do not interrupt** – Let the person finish speaking before you reply.
* **Avoid assumptions** – Don't guess what the person means. Listen and understand first.
* **Take notes when needed** – In meetings or technical discussions, write down important points.
* **Make sure everyone agrees** – Confirm that everyone has understood the discussion in the same way.

## Question 3

### What are the obstacles in your listening process?

### Answer

* **Distractions** – Noise, mobile phones, or other things can take my attention away.
* **Lack of concentration** – Sometimes I lose focus while someone is speaking.
* **Thinking about other things** – I may think about my own work or problems instead of listening.
* **Making assumptions** – I may think I already know what the person is going to say.
* **Not asking questions** – If I don't understand something, I may hesitate to ask.
* **Emotions** – Anger, stress, or excitement can make it difficult to listen properly.
* **Trying to prepare a reply** – Sometimes I focus on what I want to say next instead of listening carefully.

---

## Question 4

### What can you do to improve your listening?

### Answer

* **Pay full attention** when someone is speaking.
* **Avoid distractions** like mobile phones and unnecessary noise.
* **Do not interrupt** and let the speaker finish.
* **Maintain eye contact** to show that I am listening.
* **Take notes** during important meetings or technical discussions.
* **Ask questions** when I don't understand something.
* **Repeat important points** in my own words to check my understanding.
* **Focus on the speaker's words and feelings**, not only on my response.
* **Practice listening every day** to become a better listener.

## Question 5

### When do you switch to Passive Communication style in your day-to-day life?

### Answer

I sometimes use passive communication when I don't want to hurt someone's feelings or create an argument.

* **When I disagree with someone** but don't say what I really think.
* **When I find it difficult to say "No"** to someone.
* **When I am afraid** that the other person may get angry.
* **When I stay quiet during a discussion** even though I have something to say.
* **When I accept something I don't like** just to avoid conflict.

---

## Question 6

### When do you switch into Aggressive Communication styles in your day-to-day life?

### Answer

I may become aggressive when I am very angry, frustrated, or feel that someone is not listening to me.

* **When someone repeatedly ignores** what I am saying.
* **When I am very frustrated** with a situation.
* **When someone blames me unfairly.**
* **When I am under pressure or stressed.**
* **Sometimes I may speak loudly or use harsh words** when I am angry.

---

## Question 7

### When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive Communication styles in your day-to-day life?

### Answer

Sometimes, I use passive-aggressive communication when I am upset with someone but don't want to talk about the problem directly.

* **When someone annoys me repeatedly**, I may give sarcastic replies.
* **When I disagree with someone but don't want to argue**, I may use taunts or indirect comments.
* **When I am angry with someone**, I may stop talking to them for some time.
* Sometimes, I may say **"It's fine" or "No problem"** even when I am actually upset.
* **When I feel that someone has treated me unfairly**, I may complain about the situation to a friend instead of talking directly to that person.
* **When I don't like someone's behaviour**, I may avoid them instead of explaining what is bothering me.

## Question 8

### How can you make your communication assertive?

You can watch and analyse the videos, then think about what steps you can apply in your own life.

### Answer

I can make my communication more assertive by expressing my thoughts and feelings clearly and respectfully, without being rude or staying completely silent.

* **Speak clearly** – Say what I really think or feel.
* **Be respectful** – Respect other people's opinions even when I disagree.
* **Learn to say "No"** – I can say no politely when I cannot do something.
* **Don't interrupt** – Allow others to finish speaking before responding.
* **Control my emotions** – Take a moment to calm down before speaking when I am angry.
* **Avoid blaming** – Instead of saying *"You always do this,"* I can explain how the situation affects me.
* **Use "I" statements** – For example, *"I feel uncomfortable when this happens"* instead of *"You are always wrong."*
* **Listen to others** – Assertive communication is not only about speaking; it also means listening.
* **Be confident** – Express my opinion without being afraid, but also without forcing it on others.
* **Handle problems directly** – Instead of using sarcasm, gossip, or silent treatment, I should talk openly about the problem.

---

## Conclusion

Active listening and assertive communication help us communicate more clearly and effectively. By listening carefully, understanding others, controlling our emotions, and expressing our thoughts respectfully, we can reduce misunderstandings and build better relationships.
