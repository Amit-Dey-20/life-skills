# Good Practices for Software Development
## Question 1
## Which point(s) were new to you?
## Answer:

The points that were new to me were:

- Over-communicating with the team when there is any problem or delay.
- Collecting all questions into one message instead of sending many separate messages.
- Using screenshots, diagrams, or screen recordings to explain a problem clearly.
- Tracking my working time to improve productivity.

## Question 2
## Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?
## Answer

I think I need to improve my communication and focus.

- I will try to communicate clearly with my team and share problems or delays on time.
- When I am stuck, I will explain the problem clearly and mention what I have already tried.
- I will reduce distractions by keeping my phone silent and avoiding social media during work.
- I will follow a proper work schedule and give 100% attention to my work.
- I will regularly practice these habits to improve my communication and productivity.
