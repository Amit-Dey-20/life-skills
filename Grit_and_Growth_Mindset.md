## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

**Internal Locus of Control:**  
Internal Locus of Control means believing that my actions, choices, and efforts will play a major influence on my results, rather than blaming outside circumstances.

**Key point of the video:**  
The key point of the video is to focus on the things we can control and take responsibility for our actions instead of making excuses or blaming others. This can help us stay motivated and achieve our goals.

## Question 5

**What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?**

- Don't bound ourself.
- Accept challenges and try to solve difficult coding problems instead of avoiding them.
- Learn from mistakes and failures instead of getting discouraged.
- Practice regularly and improve step by step.
- Ask for feedback from mentors and teammates and work on their suggestions.
- Learn new technologies and concepts continuously.
- Work on small projects to apply what I learn in practice.
- Avoid comparing ourself with others and focus on own progress.
- Stay consistent and never give up when I face difficulties.
- Read documentation and understand concepts instead of depending completely on ready-made solutions.
- Track progress by noting what I learned and what I need to improve.
