## Question 3

**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

**Internal Locus of Control:**  
Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, choices, and efforts have a major influence on our results, rather than blaming outside circumstances.

**Key point of the video:**  
The key point of the video is to focus on the things we can control and take responsibility for our actions instead of making excuses or blaming others. This can help us stay motivated and achieve our goals.
