## Question 4
### Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief. Only the points, no explanation.
### Answer:

- Sleep is essential for both our brain and body.
- Good sleep improves learning, memory, and concentration.
- Lack of sleep can reduce our ability to learn and remember things.
- Deep sleep helps the brain store memories for a long time.
- Poor sleep can affect our immune system and overall health.
- Regular lack of sleep can increase the risk of serious health problems like heart attack.
- Sleep also affects our hormones, heart, and reproductive health.
- We cannot fully recover lost sleep by sleeping more later.
- Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day can improve sleep.
- A cool and comfortable bedroom can help us sleep better.
- If we cannot sleep for a long time, we should get out of bed and return when we feel sleepy.

## Question 5
### What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?
### Answer:

Some ideas I can implement to sleep better are:

- Sleep and wake up at the same time every day.
- Get at least 6-7 hours of sleep.
- Keep my room quiet and comfortable and also try to maintain the room temperature.
- Keep my bed bed clean and fresh.
- Avoid using my phone before going to bed.
- Avoid caffeine and heavy meals at night.
- maintain proper gap between dinner and going to bed.

- Avoid taking naps if they affect my night sleep.

## Question 6
### Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise. Minimum 5 points, only the points.
### Answer:
- Exercise immediately improves mood, focus, energy, and attention.
- Regular exercise can improve memory and help the brain work better.
- Exercise helps to improve important brain areas like the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex.
- Regular physical activity can help protect the brain from age-related decline and diseases like Alzheimer's.
- Exercise can make us feel happier and more energetic in our daily life.
- We do not need to go to a gym walking, climbing stairs, and other active activities can also help.
- Exercising 3–4 times a week for at least 30 minutes is a good target. Include aerobic activities that increase your heart rate.
- Making exercise a regular part of life can improve both physical and mental health.

## Question 7
### What are some steps you can take to exercise more?
### Answer:
- Take a short walk every day.
- Use stairs instead of the lift.
- Exercise for at least 30 minutes, 3–4 times a week.
- Try simple exercises like walking, jogging, cycling, or yoga.
- Take small breaks from sitting and move around.
- Exercise with friends or family to make it more enjoyable.
- Set a fixed time for exercise and make it a daily habit.
- Start with simple exercises and slowly increase the time and intensity.
- For remainder do exercise after daily basic activity example, after cleaning teeth.
- To maintain regularity and consistency add exercise as a tiny habits.
