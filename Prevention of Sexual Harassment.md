# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question 1
### What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
### Answer:

Sexual harassment can be caused by unwelcome behaviour, words, gestures, or actions of a sexual nature.

Some examples are:

- Making jokes, comments, or suggestive remarks.
- Sharing inappropriate pictures, posters, cartoons, emails, or messages at work.
- Repeatedly asking someone for a date after they have said no.
- Unwanted touching, hugging, or other physical contact.
- Making sexual gestures or comments about someone's body or appearance.
- Continuing any behaviour after the person has clearly shown that they are uncomfortable.



## Question 2
### What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?
### Answer:

- I would first identify the behaviour and understand that it is not acceptable.

- If I feel comfortable, I would clearly tell the person to stop the behaviour.

- If the person continues, I would seek help from my supervisor, HR, or another trusted person.

- If the person harassing me is my supervisor, I would report the matter to their supervisor or HR.

- I would follow the organization's complaint or grievance procedure.

- I would keep a record of the incident, especially if the behaviour happens repeatedly.

- If I witness someone else being harassed, I would support them and encourage them to report it.

- I would not ignore repeated or serious incidents and would take appropriate action to help maintain a safe and respectful workplace.
